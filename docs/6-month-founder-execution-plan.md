# YuvaHive — 6-Month Commercialisation Execution Plan

> One business that can eventually create five revenue streams. Win the college
> placement wedge first. Gamification keeps students engaged; **employability is
> the reason colleges buy.**

## 1. Strategic thesis

- **One business, not five.** Put all capital and focus behind YuvaHive for 6 months.
- **Buyer = college. User = student.** The college pays for placement outcomes; students use it because it is engaging and useful.
- **Wedge = daily coding quiz → measurable placement readiness.** This is the proof the college needs.
- **Advantage we already have:** technical ability, student access, a team, and an existing brand.
- **Sequencing:** prove outcomes with a few colleges → convert to paid → build a repeatable sales engine → stack new revenue streams on the same user base.

## 2. What we are actually selling

**To colleges (the customer):**
> "A placement-readiness program that measurably improves your students' coding
> proficiency and interview readiness — with cohort-level dashboards and
> baseline/endline proof."

Not: "a gamified quiz app."

**To students (the users):**
- Daily 5-minute coding quiz on WhatsApp.
- Streaks, leaderboards, XP, college-vs-college competition (engagement layer).
- Visible readiness score that maps to placement preparedness.

**Pricing model (initial):**
- 30-day free pilot with baseline + endline assessment.
- Convert to annual license: **₹150–₹300 per student / year**, minimum contract ₹40k–₹75k per college.
- Discount for early cohort in exchange for a case study and testimonial.

## 3. Five revenue streams (sequenced, not simultaneous)

| # | Stream | Start | Role in plan |
|---|--------|-------|--------------|
| 1 | College placement-readiness licenses | Month 1 | **Primary revenue.** Prove and repeat. |
| 2 | Sponsored coding challenges / brand sponsorships | Month 4 | Upside on existing engagement. |
| 3 | Certification & proctored assessments | Month 5 | Monetise assessments colleges already pay for. |
| 4 | Recruiter / hiring pipeline access | Month 6 | Long-term network effect. |
| 5 | Premium student subscription | Month 6+ | Optional, low priority. |

Do **not** start streams 2–5 until stream 1 has 10+ paying colleges.

## 5. Month-by-month plan

### Month 0 — Foundation (2 weeks)
- Finalise pitch, pilot offer, and pricing sheet.
- Build the minimum college-facing dashboard + baseline/endline assessment.
- Prepare pilot agreement (simple 1-page MOU), data/privacy note, onboarding checklist.
- List 100 target colleges (tier-2/3 engineering colleges; MP + nearby states first).
- Set up CRM (even a spreadsheet): college, TPO, stage, next action, owner.

### Month 1 — Prove it (free pilots)
- **Goal:** 3 pilot colleges live, 500 activated students.
- Run baseline assessment, launch daily quiz on WhatsApp, weekly leaderboard.
- Run endline assessment at day 30; produce a 1-page outcome report per college.
- **Revenue:** ₹0 (deliberate).
- **Success gate:** ≥40% weekly active students and a measurable readiness improvement.

### Month 2 — First revenue
- **Goal:** convert 3 pilots to paid; 3 paid colleges; 1,000 students.
- **Revenue target:** ₹1,00,000 collected.
- Turn the best pilot into a case study (before/after readiness, participation, quotes).
- Tighten onboarding to <1 day per college.

### Month 3 — Repeatable sales engine
- **Goal:** 10 colleges total; 3,000 students.
- **Revenue target:** ₹2,50,000 cumulative.
- Standardise pilot → paid conversion; build a 5-email/WhatsApp outreach sequence.
- Add college-vs-college leaderboard to strengthen the engagement story.

### Month 4 — Scale + first sponsorship
- **Goal:** 18 colleges; 6,000 students.
- **Revenue target:** ₹5,00,000 cumulative.
- Launch sponsored challenge pilot with one brand (target ₹50k–₹1L).
- Hire/assign a part-time sales/onboarding support if pipeline > 30 active colleges.

### Month 5 — Assessments & certifications
- **Goal:** 25 colleges; 9,000 students.
- **Revenue target:** ₹8,00,000 cumulative.
- Launch paid certification / proctored assessment add-on.
- Begin renewal conversations with Month 1–2 colleges.

### Month 6 — Hiring pipeline + renewals
- **Goal:** 35 colleges; 12,000 students; renewals started.
- **Revenue target:** ₹12,00,000 cumulative; ₹2–3L monthly run-rate.
- Pilot recruiter access with 2–3 hiring partners.
- Decide: raise, bootstrap, or expand — based on proven unit economics.

## 7. KPIs we manage weekly

- **Sales:** colleges contacted, meetings booked, pilots started, pilots converted, CAC.
- **Activation:** students onboarded, first-quiz completion rate.
- **Engagement:** weekly active students (WAS), 4-week retention, streak length.
- **Outcomes:** baseline vs endline readiness score, % students improving.
- **Revenue:** collected revenue, pipeline value, renewal intent.
- **Product:** answer accuracy, uptime, delivery failures.

## 8. Product roadmap required to sell

Priority order (only what the sales motion needs):
1. College cohort dashboard (participation, readiness, top/bottom performers).
2. Baseline and endline assessment with auto-generated report.
3. Multi-college accounts and per-college leaderboards.
4. Certificates (PDF) for participation and assessments.
5. CRM-friendly exports (CSV) and weekly automated email reports.
6. Reliability: uptime monitoring, retry logic for WhatsApp delivery.

## 10. Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Colleges see it as "just a quiz app" | Lead every conversation with placement outcomes and the outcome report. |
| Low student activation | WhatsApp-first delivery, 5-minute format, college leaderboard, TPO nudges. |
| Long college sales cycles | Start with TPO/HOD, offer free pilot, keep contract small and simple. |
| Answer/quality errors | Review process + automated checks before publishing. |
| Founder bandwidth split across businesses | Explicit 6-month commitment; no unrelated ventures. |
| Churn after pilot | Endline report + renewal offer before pilot ends. |
